Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 (updated for Report and Ask)  ·  MSRX Gauge 1.0

The short version

MSRX Gauge collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. It has no network permission at all, so it cannot connect to the internet.

What the app reads

To show how your computer is doing, the app reads these figures from the system, on your computer only:

  • Processor use, overall and per core.
  • Memory use, memory pressure and swap use.
  • How many bytes your network connections have sent and received in total. It does not see what was sent, to whom, or which websites or apps were involved.
  • The names, size and free space of disks you can see in the file browser, and how many bytes disks have read and written.
  • Battery level, charging state, time remaining, cycle count and capacity compared with new.
  • Graphics processor use and the system’s thermal state.
  • Facts about the computer itself: the chip, model identifier, number of cores, installed memory, system version and the time since it was last restarted.
  • Whether MSRX Process Explorer is installed, so it can show a button that opens it.

It does not read your files, documents, messages, contacts, photos, browsing, location or the list of apps you run.

What is kept, and where

  • Readings are kept in memory to draw the charts: every reading for the last 60 seconds, and 10-second averages of processor, memory, battery and thermal readings for the last hour, plus the highest readings since the app started. They are gone when the app quits. Nothing is written to disk unless you save a report.
  • Reports are created only when you open Report. A report is saved only to a file you choose in the save dialog (PDF or image), or copied to your clipboard as text if you choose Copy. The app can write only to the file you pick there.
  • Ask conversations are kept in memory only, and are gone when you press Clear or quit the app.
  • Your settings and alert thresholds are saved in the app’s own preferences on your computer.

Ask, the on-device assistant

Ask answers questions using the language model built into your computer’s operating system, which runs on your computer. Your question and a summary of the current readings are given to that model on your computer; the app does not send them anywhere and does not use any cloud model. Ask works only where the system’s on-device model is available and turned on. Answers are generated by the model and can be wrong.

Notifications and login

If you turn on an alert, the app asks the system for permission to show notifications. Alerts are created on your computer by the app and shown by the system; they are not sent through any server. If you turn on Open at login, the app adds itself to your login items; turning it off removes it.

No network, no tracking

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code. Nothing is sold, rented or shared, because nothing is collected. The Support, Privacy and Terms links in the About window open these pages in your web browser; the app itself does not connect.

On the App Store this app’s privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it reads or keeps, this page and its effective date will change with it.
